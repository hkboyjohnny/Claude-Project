## Data quality notes

- **Primary (downloaded filings):** all COFCO group, Joycome, Mengniu, ADM and Bunge figures.
- **Secondary (press reports of company data):** COFCO International (it publishes no full accounts; profit is not disclosed), LDC, Wilmar history, pork peers, Yili, raw milk.
- **Compiled from annual reports, not re-downloaded for this pack:** ADM/Bunge/Wilmar/LDC 2021-2023, Mengniu and Yili 2019-2022 revenue and profit, China soybean imports 2019-2023. These are widely published figures but were not checked against a saved document here.
- **Analyst judgement:** the cycle-map scores and the 2027E view.
