**To:** Shanghai Branch Compliance
**Cc:** [Relationship Manager] / [Credit] / [Legal]
**Subject:** Feasibility enquiry – Cross-border direct loan to Leoni AG (Luxshare subsidiary) to refinance shareholder loan

Dear Compliance colleagues,

We would like your preliminary view on a proposed **cross-border direct loan (境外贷款)** from Shanghai Branch to **Leoni AG** (Germany). Leoni AG has been acquired by **Luxshare Precision Industry Co., Ltd. (立讯精密)**: 50.1% at closing on 10 Jul 2025, raised to 74.9% in Apr 2026. Luxshare announced that all PRC ODI approvals and filings were obtained (announcements 2024-075 and 2025-092).

**Background and use of proceeds**

1. **Proposed use:** The loan would refinance an existing **shareholder loan from Luxshare** to Leoni AG. Per announcement 2024-075, the lender is **Luxshare Precision Limited (Hong Kong) or its affiliates**, which are offshore entities.
2. **Use of the shareholder loan:** The shareholder loan was used to **repay Leoni's syndicated loan closed in 2023**. This is confirmed by company disclosure and by legal counsel. Luxshare announcement 2024-075 states the related-party loans are for prepaying Leoni debt triggered by the change of control, including revolving credit facilities, Schuldschein and factoring. Deal counsel (Clifford Chance) confirmed that a substantial part of Leoni's liabilities were refinanced through Luxshare at closing.
3. **Use of the syndicated loan:** The 2023 syndicated loan supported Leoni's **working capital needs**. Leoni's annual report describes these facilities as "syndicated working capital credit lines" of EUR 684m, extended to end-2026 under the 2023 restructuring. The facility agreement will be provided.
4. **No dividends:** According to the client, **no part of the shareholder loan was used for dividends** or other shareholder distributions. No Leoni dividend is publicly disclosed. The SPA prohibited profit distributions before closing, and Leoni AG made a net loss of about EUR 128m in 2023. We will obtain a written representation and audited financial statements to confirm this.

Key terms (indicative): [Currency] [Amount], [Tenor], [Credit support: standalone / Luxshare guarantee / keepwell].

**Questions for Compliance**

1. **Eligibility and route:** Is Leoni AG an eligible borrower for Shanghai Branch's overseas lending? Should the loan be booked as RMB overseas lending (银发〔2022〕27号) or FX overseas lending? Is there headroom under the macro-prudential overseas lending cap?
2. **Repatriation concern (key point):** The shareholder-loan lender appears to be Luxshare's **Hong Kong** subsidiary, so the repayment would stay offshore. Please confirm whether this is acceptable. If the Hong Kong lender was itself funded from onshore Luxshare (via 境外放款 or ODI funds), would the repayment be viewed as indirect repatriation of overseas loan proceeds to the PRC? If it is restricted, would any of these be acceptable:
   - (a) payment to a Luxshare offshore entity;
   - (b) partial refinancing;
   - (c) restricting proceeds to working capital or third-party debt?
3. **Guarantee:** If Luxshare provides a guarantee, this becomes 内保外贷 (汇发〔2014〕29号). Would the repayment of the shareholder loan conflict with its use-of-funds restrictions? Would you prefer a standalone loan or a keepwell structure?
4. **Documentation:** Beyond the items below, what evidence do you need to support the use-of-proceeds review?
   - Luxshare ODI filings and SAFE registration (including the overseas lending registration for the shareholder loan, if applicable).
   - Shareholder loan agreement and fund-flow tracing to the syndicate repayment.
   - 2023 syndicated facility agreement and evidence of working capital use.
   - Counsel confirmation.
   - Client no-dividend representation and audited financials.

**背景及资金用途**

1. **拟议用途：**本笔贷款拟用于置换立讯精密向 Leoni AG 提供的**股东借款**。根据立讯精密公告 2024-075，出借方为**香港立讯（Luxshare Precision Limited）或其关联企业**，均为境外主体。
2. **股东借款用途：**该股东借款用于**偿还 Leoni 于2023年签署的银团贷款**，已经公司公开披露及律师确认。公告 2024-075 载明，关联方贷款「用于提前偿还因本交易触发的 Leoni 集团的任何相关财务债务（包括但不限于现有的循环信贷额度、本票债券、保理和反向保理、其他本地贷款）」。
3. **银团贷款用途：**该2023年银团贷款用于支持 Leoni 的**营运资金需求**。Leoni 年报将其列为 6.84 亿欧元的“银团营运资金授信额度”，并已于2023年重组中展期至2026年底。相关协议将由客户提供。
