# Public Disclosures — Evidence Pack (Leoni AG / Luxshare)

Compiled 9 Nov 2026 from public sources. Chinese quotes are verbatim from the filings, with English translations below them. Items marked **[not found]** were searched for but are not publicly disclosed; they must come from the client.

## A. Summary against the key questions

| Question | What the public record shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Was the shareholder loan used to repay debt?** | **Yes.** Luxshare committed in the SPA to provide **related-party loans** to the Leoni group, so Leoni could **prepay financial debt whose repayment was triggered by the change of control**: revolving credit facilities, promissory notes / Schuldschein, factoring and reverse factoring, and other local loans. At closing (10 Jul 2025), "a substantial part of LEONI's financial liabilities were refinanced through Luxshare". Leoni's restructured note liabilities were fully repaid. | A1, A3, A4 |
| **Who is the shareholder-loan lender?** | **Luxshare Precision Limited (香港立讯, Hong Kong) or its affiliates**, not the PRC listed parent. | A1 |
| **Was the shareholder loan used for dividends?** | **No public evidence of any dividend.** Before closing, the SPA prohibited capital reductions and profit distributions. Leoni AG made a net loss of EUR -128m in 2023, and its share capital was cut to zero in the 2023 StaRUG plan, leaving no distributable profit. **[Leoni FY2024/FY2025 dividend confirmation not found publicly — obtain audited FS + client rep]** | A1, A6 |
| **What did the 2023 syndicated loan fund?** | Leoni's 2024 management report describes **"syndicated working capital credit lines" of EUR 684m** (of which EUR 5m usable only for guarantees) plus **Schuldschein of EUR 172m**. The 2023 StaRUG restructuring extended both to **end-2026**. In other words, the facilities were working capital lines. | A5, A6 |
| **Amount of the shareholder loan / repayment detail** | **[not found]** Neither Luxshare's 2025 interim nor its 2025 annual report breaks out the intra-group loan, which is eliminated on consolidation. | A2, A7 |

## B. Sources and key extracts

### A1. Luxshare announcement 2024-075 (13/14 Sep 2024): 《关于收购 Leoni AG 及其下属全资子公司股权的公告》
Link: <https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-09-14/1221224368.PDF>

**Deal structure**
- 新加坡立讯 (Luxshare Precision Singapore Pte. Ltd.) acquires 50.1% of Leoni AG for **EUR 205.41m**.
- 新加坡汇聚 (Time Interconnect Singapore, owned 51% by 香港立讯 and 49% by 汇聚科技) acquires 100% of Leoni Kabel GmbH (ACS) **from Leoni AG** for **EUR 320m**. The ACS proceeds therefore go into Leoni AG itself.
- Funding: 「本次收购资金来源均为公司全资子公司及控股子公司的自筹资金」 ("all acquisition funds come from the company's wholly owned and controlled subsidiaries' own funds").

**Financing arrangements (WSD SPA), verbatim:**
> 「香港立讯和买方坚守并承诺，将会管理因执行交易和相关控制权变更所引发的后果，并会支持莱尼集团的融资结构。因此，香港立讯及买方将促使其关联企业：（i）以关联方贷款的形式，遵循移转定价规定，向 Leoni 集团及 ACS 实体提供所需资金（在购买价格和任何 IC 融资应收账款购买价格总和之外），用于提前偿还因本交易触发的 Leoni 集团的任何相关财务债务（包括但不限于现有的循环信贷额度、本票债券、保理和反向保理、其他本地贷款）……(ii) 就 Leoni 集团和 ACS 实体的持续经营而言，协助 LEONI 的财务团队获得新的融资，并以关联方贷款的形式向 Leoni 集团和 ACS 实体提供资金支持。」

Translation: Luxshare HK and the buyer commit to manage the consequences of the change of control and to support Leoni's financing structure. Their affiliates will (i) provide related-party loans, on transfer-pricing terms and in addition to the purchase price, so that Leoni can prepay the financial debt triggered by the transaction, including the existing revolving credit facilities, promissory notes, factoring and reverse factoring, and other local loans; and (ii) help Leoni's finance team raise new financing for ongoing operations, and fund Leoni through related-party loans.

**Financing arrangements (ACS SPA), verbatim:**
> 「……香港立讯或其关联公司将签署一份相关方贷款协议，在交易完成时为 LEONI 集团(包括 ACS 及 WSD)的相关实体提供信用额度，其中，ACS 交易项下的交易价金将用于抵减需提供的 LEONI 融资支持。」

Translation: Luxshare HK or its affiliates will sign a related-party loan agreement giving Leoni group entities a credit line at closing. The ACS sale proceeds will reduce the amount of Leoni financing support required.

**No distributions before closing (ACS seller covenant), verbatim:**
> 「(v) 不进行任何减资或利润分配（除非为重组前步骤所需/权宜的情况）」

Translation: no capital reduction or profit distribution, except as needed for the pre-closing reorganisation steps.

> 「本次交易为境外投资，本次交易需要办理境内国家商务部备案、国家发改委境外投资备案和外汇管理登记」

Translation: this is an outbound investment and requires MOFCOM filing, NDRC ODI filing and SAFE registration.

### A2. Luxshare announcement 2025-092 (10 Jul 2025): progress / closing announcement
Link: <https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20250710201444926961880> (also on cninfo)
- 「公司已悉数取得与本次交易项下ACS业务及WSD业务拟交割主体相关的境内外监管机构审批、审查及备案等必要程序。」 Translation: all required PRC and foreign regulatory approvals, reviews and filings have been obtained. This covers the ODI filings.
- On 10 Jul 2025, Luxshare paid the ACS consideration to Leoni AG (via 新加坡汇聚) and the 50.1% consideration to L2-Beteiligungs (via 新加坡立讯).
- Leoni's Indian subsidiary was carved out pending Indian approval.
- The announcement says nothing on the loan amount.

### A6. Leoni 2024 management report: financing section (via filings aggregator; the primary PDF could not be retrieved automatically)
Link: <https://financialreports.eu/filings/leoni-ag/annual-report/2025/7979447/>
Paraphrase from German: at year-end 2024 the group had **syndicated working capital credit lines of EUR 684m** (prior year EUR 684m; EUR 5m each usable only for guarantees) and **Schuldschein loans of EUR 172m** (prior year EUR 171m), **both maturing end-2026**. In mid-2023, under the restructuring concept, the existing financing agreements were extended to end-2026 and adjusted. Leoni AG and group companies granted collateral to the lenders.

**2023 StaRUG restructuring background**
- Plan accepted 31 May 2023 and confirmed by the Nuremberg restructuring court on 21 Jun 2023. Appeals were dismissed on 17 Jul 2023.
- Debt relief of about **EUR 708m**, plus **EUR 150m** of new equity from Stefan Pierer. Share capital was reduced to zero and Leoni delisted.
- Leoni's RCF facility was EUR 750m.
- Sources: [Leoni press release – required majorities](https://www.leoni.com/en/press/releases/details/concept-leoni0/) · [Kirkland & Ellis (RCF lenders)](https://www.kirkland.com/news/press-release/2023/07/kirkland-advises-lenders-under-leoni-ags-rcf-facility-on-groundbreaking-restructuring) · [Gleiss Lutz](https://www.gleisslutz.com/en/Gleiss-Lutz_LEONI_StaRUG_Juli_2023_en.html) · [Freshfields (lenders)](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/news/news-search/2023/07/freshfields-advises-lenders-of-leoni-ag-on-financial-starug-restructuring)
